Field experience · Student READY

RAWE · Rural Agricultural Work Experience

Village-level fieldwork in Karsara, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The photographs below document the activities and practical learning from my rural visits.

Village information collection and Panchayat visit

Collected basic village information from the Panchayat and met the Pradhan of Karsara to understand the village context.

Farmers’ survey and farm visits

Visited farms and interacted with farmers to document their practices, cropping choices, and field-level observations.

Village mapping with schoolchildren

Worked with children at the local school to prepare a village map as part of community-level fieldwork.

Learning agricultural practices

Observed local agricultural practices and learned how farmers manage crops and farm operations.

ZECC construction and use demonstration

Participated in a demonstration on the construction and use of a Zero Energy Cool Chamber (ZECC).

Village transect

Conducted a village transect to observe local resources, land use, and features across different parts of the village.

Neemashtra preparation demonstration

Prepared Neemashtra as part of a demonstration on plant-based, organic pest management.

Agricultural awareness and farmer meetings

Supported awareness activities and discussions on agri-apps, government schemes, soil health, pesticide overuse, contingency planning, and sustainable farming practices.

Gaushala, poultry farm, and livestock awareness

Visited a village Gaushala and poultry farm and took part in awareness activities related to cattle vaccination and shed sanitation.

Taxonomy of local trees

Observed and documented local tree species as part of learning about their identification and taxonomy.

Soil testing demonstrations

Practised the soil drop and cotton swab methods during soil-testing activities.

Women’s health and hygiene awareness

Participated in group discussions on women’s health, hygiene, and balanced diets for adult women.

Supplementary foods and child health awareness

Demonstrated vitamin A- and iron-rich food preparation for preschool children and supported awareness on child health, hygiene, and cleanliness.

Kokedama and plant-gifting activity

Learned to prepare kokedama and presented plants, a kokedama, and a terrarium to the host farmer’s family as a token of gratitude.

Rose plantation visit

Visited a rose plantation to observe cultivation and plantation-level practices.

Local textile weaving unit visit

Visited a local textile weaving unit to learn about a village-based, non-farm livelihood activity.

Nursery visit and operations

Visited a nursery to understand its operations, maintenance practices, and common challenges.
